Transparency & Realistic SEO Expectations

SEO takes time, and we won't promise otherwise. We don't guarantee specific rankings, and we avoid fixed timeline promises, since every website's starting point is different. What we do offer is clear communication and meaningful reporting, so you understand what's happening and why. Depending on your goals, reporting may reference metrics such as organic traffic, keyword visibility or movement, engagement, and conversions where relevant, though we don't promise that these will always trend upward. Frequently Asked Questions

Is RankNepal only for large businesses?
No. We work with businesses of different sizes, and the right starting point usually depends on your current website and goals rather than your business size.
Do you work with businesses outside Kathmandu?
Yes. We work with businesses across Nepal, not only those based in Kathmandu.
Do you work with businesses outside Nepal?
Our primary focus is on businesses targeting the Nepali market or Nepali audiences, so our approach is built around that context.
How long does SEO take to show results?
It varies. Timelines depend on factors like competition, your website's current condition, existing content, search demand, authority, technical foundation, and the effort put into ongoing work, so we avoid promising a fixed timeframe.
Do you guarantee rankings?
No. No honest SEO provider can guarantee specific rankings, since search engines control their own algorithms. We focus on sound, transparent practices instead.
How do I get started?
The easiest way to get started is by requesting a free SEO audit, which gives us a clear starting picture of your website.
What's the difference between a free SEO audit and ongoing SEO work?
A free SEO audit is a one-time review that identifies issues and opportunities. Ongoing SEO work involves implementing changes, monitoring results, and adjusting strategy over time.
What makes SEO different when targeting the Nepali market?
Search behavior, competition levels, and local search patterns in Nepal can differ from other markets, so strategies need to reflect how people in Nepal actually search rather than assuming a generic approach will work the same way.
