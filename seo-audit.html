What Is an SEO Audit?

An SEO audit is a structured review of your website that examines how well it currently performs from a search engine's perspective. It looks at technical health, on-page elements, content quality, and search visibility to build a clear picture of where your website stands. Rather than guessing where to start, an SEO audit gives you a practical, evidence-based foundation before any optimization work begins.

Why an SEO Audit Matters

Without a proper audit, SEO work can end up addressing the wrong problems or missing issues entirely. An audit can reveal technical problems, indexing or crawl issues, on-page weaknesses, content gaps, internal linking problems, and local SEO issues, along with genuine opportunities for improvement. This makes it easier to prioritize effort on what will actually make a difference, rather than working blindly.

What We Check in an SEO Audit

  • Technical SEO

    Reviewing the underlying structure of your website, including how well search engines can access and process it.

  • Crawlability & Indexing

    Checking whether search engines can properly crawl your pages and whether your important pages are actually indexed.

  • Page Titles & Meta Descriptions

    Assessing whether your titles and descriptions clearly and accurately represent each page's content.

  • Heading Structure

    Reviewing your use of H1 through H3 headings for a logical, easy-to-follow content hierarchy.

  • Content Quality & Search Intent

    Evaluating whether your content genuinely answers what your potential customers are searching for.

  • Internal Linking

    Checking how well your pages link to one another and whether important pages are easy to reach.

  • URL Structure

    Reviewing whether your URLs are clean, descriptive, and consistent across the website.

  • Mobile-Friendliness & Site Speed

    Checking how your website performs on mobile devices and how quickly your pages load.

  • Local SEO Signals

    Reviewing local SEO elements such as Google Business Profile setup and listing consistency, where relevant to your business.

What Results to Expect

An SEO audit itself doesn't change your rankings; it identifies what's currently helping or holding back your website's search performance. The value comes from acting on the findings over time. We do not guarantee specific rankings, traffic, or leads as a result of an audit, since outcomes depend on the work carried out afterward and factors beyond our control.
