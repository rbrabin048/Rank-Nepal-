How We Approach SEO Projects

Before starting any SEO work, we take time to understand the business and website we're working with. This typically involves looking at:

  • Business goals
  • Target audience
  • Search intent
  • Technical SEO
  • Keyword opportunities
  • Content
  • Local search visibility
  • Competition
  • Measurement

The examples below illustrate how these considerations come together for different types of businesses. They are presented as illustrative project approaches, not as claimed client results.

Illustrative Project Examples

As a Nepal-focused SEO company, we've outlined the following examples to show how an SEO project might be approached for different business types. These are illustrative scenarios based on common challenges, not fabricated client results, screenshots, or testimonials.

Local Service Business — SEO Visibility Improvement Plan

Challenge: Weak local visibility, incomplete location targeting, and limited service pages made it difficult for the business to appear in relevant local searches.

Approach: Local keyword research, Google Business Profile considerations, location and service page optimization, internal linking improvements, and technical cleanup.

Measurement: Impressions, clicks, relevant keyword visibility, and enquiries or conversions.

Illustrative project example — not a claimed client result.

E-commerce Website — Organic Search Growth Strategy

Challenge: Category and product pages weren't targeting useful search intent, limiting organic visibility for relevant product searches.

Approach: Keyword mapping, category page optimization, product and content improvements, internal linking, and technical SEO review.

Measurement: Organic clicks, impressions, rankings or visibility, and conversions.

Illustrative project example — not a claimed client result.

Professional Services Website — Technical & Content SEO

Challenge: Technical issues and content that didn't fully match search intent were limiting the website's search performance.

Approach: Technical audit, indexing and crawl checks, on-page optimization, content restructuring, and internal linking improvements.

Measurement: Indexing health, organic visibility, clicks, engagement, and conversions.

Illustrative project example — not a claimed client result.

Hospitality & Tourism — Local Search Strategy

Challenge: Strong competition for destination and service-related searches, inconsistent location information, and content that didn't fully address traveler search intent.

Approach: Local SEO improvements, location-focused content, consistent business listing information, on-page optimization for service and destination pages, and internal linking between related pages.

Measurement: Local visibility, impressions and clicks for destination-related searches, and direct enquiries.

Illustrative project example — not a claimed client result.

Why SEO Results Vary

The examples above are meant to illustrate methodology, not to suggest a fixed outcome. Actual results depend on a website's starting condition, industry, competition, search demand, technical foundation, content quality, and the consistency of implementation over time. We don't guarantee specific rankings, traffic, or conversions, since these depend on many factors beyond any single SEO effort.
